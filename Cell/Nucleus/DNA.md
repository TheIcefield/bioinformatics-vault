Deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA) is a substance contained in all cells that serves as a template for all cellular activity.
Consists of four nucleobases [[adenine]], [[cytosine]], [[guanine]] and [[thymine]], whose order determines its primary structure.
Consists of 2 [strands](Strand), running in opposite directions.
```
   AAAA...GGGCCT
   AGGCCC...TTTT
```
